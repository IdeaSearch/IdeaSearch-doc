01 / 定义Definition

一层的表示，
用两个平行的坐标描述。

Describe one layer's representation
with two parallel coordinates.

沿着残差流，Transformer 是一个沿深度演化的离散动力系统。在某一层，一篇文档的全部 token 表示是高维空间里的一团点云。我们对它只问两个互相独立的问题：文档之间区别有多大，文档本身有多复杂。

Through its residual stream, a Transformer is a discrete dynamical system along depth. At one layer, all token representations of a document form a cloud in a high-dimensional space. We ask it two independent questions only: how different documents are from one another, and how complex each document is in itself.

TOKEN CLOUD一篇文档 = 一团点云A document = a cloud

$\delta$：这篇文档的均值相对其他文档均值（十字）的偏移；$C$：这篇文档 token 的协方差（虚线椭圆），刻画点云的形状。$\delta$: the offset of this document's mean from the mean of the other documents (cross); $C$: the covariance of this document's tokens (dashed ellipse), the shape of the cloud.

$u$ · DISTINGUISHABILITY文档之间区别有多大How different documents are$u = \dfrac{|\delta|^2}{\sqrt{\operatorname{tr} C^2}}$

文档均值的偏移 $\delta$，相对于文档内的散布。$u$ 越大，越容易仅凭一个 token 认出它来自哪篇文档。The offset $\delta$ of the document mean, relative to the spread within. The larger $u$, the easier it is to tell a token's document from that token alone.

$d_C$ · DIMENSION文档本身有多复杂How complex a document is$d_C = \dfrac{(\operatorname{tr} C)^2}{\operatorname{tr} C^2}$

文档内的变化有效地铺在多少个方向上：均匀铺在 $k$ 个方向时 $d_C = k$，介于 1 与嵌入维数 $D$ 之间。Over how many directions the variation within the document spreads: evenly over $k$ directions gives $d_C = k$, between 1 and the embedding dimension $D$.

02 / 相图Phase diagram

工作相在中央，
漂移相通向四个极限。

The working phase sits in the middle;
the drifting phase leads to four limits.

把训练后模型与未训练网络深层的状态放在一起，($u$, $d_C$) 平面分成两个区域：训练后模型停留的工作相，和未训练网络漂向的漂移相。漂移相通向四个极限，最常见的是左上的噪声区和右下的坍缩区。

Pool the deep-layer states of trained models and untrained networks, and the ($u$, $d_C$) plane splits into two regions: the working phase where trained models stay, and the drifting phase toward which untrained networks drift. The drifting phase leads to four limits; the most common are the noise region at top left and the collapse region at bottom right.

03 / 动力学Dynamics

运动趋势随状态而变：
一个差分方程决定多层的行为。

The tendency to move depends on the state:
one difference equation sets the multilayer behavior.

只看位置分不清两种情况：有些未训练的推理流也会穿过工作相，只是不停留。所以我们把逐层的位移写成状态的函数——下一层怎么走，由这一层在哪里、上一层怎么走决定。

Position alone cannot tell two cases apart: some untrained flows also pass through the working phase, without staying. So we write the layer-to-layer step as a function of the state — how the next layer moves is set by where this layer is and how the previous one moved.

$v_{t+1} = \alpha\, v_t + F(x_t) + \eta_t$
$\alpha$惯性：上一层的位移有多少带到下一层inertia: how much of the last step carries over$F$有效力：只由当前位置决定的平均位移effective force: the mean step set by the current position$\eta$涨落：其余自由度留下的无偏扰动fluctuation: the unbiased remainder

$x_t$：第 $t$ 层在相图上的位置；$v_t = x_t - x_{t-1}$$x_t$: the position of layer $t$ on the diagram; $v_t = x_t - x_{t-1}$

t = 0

04 / 训练Training

训练改写差分方程，
把推理流拉进工作相。

Training rewrites the difference equation
and pulls the inference flow into the working phase.

以训练步数为控制参数，在 Pythia-1.4B 的 19 个检查点上测量同一个网络。按下播放，看深层状态怎样随训练进入工作相、惯性怎样随之改变。

With the training step as the control parameter, we measure the same network at 19 checkpoints of Pythia-1.4B. Press play to watch the deep-layer states enter the working phase and the inertia change with them.

第 256 到 512 步之间，推理流进入工作相Between steps 256 and 512 the flow enters the working phase

工作相占比跳变的同时，惯性骤降到 0 附近——按两相比例加权（杠杆定则）即可再现。我们把这次转变解释为一次相变，与训练中已报道的突变一致，例如 grokking 中泛化的出现、induction head 的形成。As the working fraction jumps, the inertia drops to near 0 — reproduced by weighting the two phases by their fractions (the lever rule). We interpret this as a phase transition, consistent with abrupt changes reported during training, such as the onset of generalization in grokking and the formation of induction heads.
